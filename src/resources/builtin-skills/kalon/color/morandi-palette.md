# Morandi Palette System SOP (莫兰迪沉静色彩系统)

The Morandi color system relies on low-saturation, gray-infused tones that convey understated sophistication, visual calm, and high aesthetic cohesion.

## 1. Core Principles
- **Muted Saturation**: Limit HSL saturation to 15% - 40% across background and secondary accents.
- **Warm Gray Undertones**: Infuse a touch of warm beige or cool stone gray into pure whites and blacks (e.g. `#1b1c1d`, `#ebeef2`).
- **Harmonic Contrast**: Avoid pure `#000000` or `#ffffff`. Use nuanced shades: `#2c3539`, `#f4f3ef`, `#dcd8cf`.

## 2. Standard Tonal Swatches
- **Earth / Warm Ochre**: `#c4a482`, `#b8977e`, `#9e7d62`
- **Sage / Dust Green**: `#8a9a86`, `#748570`, `#5b6c57`
- **Dusty Rose / Muted Mauve**: `#b68d94`, `#9f767d`, `#855f65`
- **Slate / Storm Blue**: `#768a96`, `#60737f`, `#4b5c68`
- **Stone / Mist Gray**: `#d5d5d0`, `#b8b8b0`, `#9a9a92`

## 3. Application Guidelines
- Backgrounds take the most muted tints (light mode: `#f8f7f4`; dark mode: `#181a1b`).
- Foreground text uses deep slate or warm charcoal (`#23272a`, `#e4e6eb`).
- Accents should not scream; they guide the eye with subtle elegance.
