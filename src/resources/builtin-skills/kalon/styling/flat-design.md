# Modern Flat Design SOP (现代扁平极简风与负空间节奏)

## 1. Principles of Elimination
- Remove unnecessary bevels, heavy 3D gradients, and decorative noise.
- Let typography, whitespace, and color blocks define spatial relationships.

## 2. Typography Hierarchy
- Strict 1.25 or 1.33 ratio type scales (e.g. 12px, 14px, 16px, 20px, 28px).
- Generous line heights (1.5 - 1.7) for body text; tight line heights (1.1 - 1.2) for display titles.

## 3. Negative Space Rhythm
- Generous negative space communicates confidence and structural clarity.
- Micro whitespace (between icon and label): 4-8px.
- Macro whitespace (between sections): 24-48px.
