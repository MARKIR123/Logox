# Text & Narrative Polishing SOP (文学文案润色与叙事语气塑造)

## 1. Cadence and Sentence Economy
- Cut hollow adjectives, AI throat-clearing, and verbose filler words.
- Alternate sentence lengths to create acoustic rhythm (staccato punchy lines mixed with flowing clauses).

## 2. Sensory Concreteness
- Replace abstract generalities with tangible physical nouns and kinetic verbs.
- Avoid clichés: instead of "it was chaotic", describe the specific clattering of coffee mugs and flickering terminal monitors.

## 3. Character Voice Consistency
- Ensure characters speak from their social origins, technical worldview, and emotional subtext.
- In documentation: prioritize quiet elegance, clarity, and precision over hollow promotional grandstanding.
