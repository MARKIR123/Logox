# Deep Research & Synthesis SOP (深度调研与方案综合流)

## 1. Fact Gathering (Aletheia)
- Dispatch Aletheia with specific research hypotheses or investigative questions.
- Aletheia fetches clean markdown using `WebFetch` or inspects local codebase / journals.
- Record facts with exact URL sources or file coordinates. Never synthesize speculative prose without citation.

## 2. Synthesis & Decision (Logox)
- Director Logox personally reviews gathered facts; no lazy re-delegation of synthesis.
- Formulate concrete trade-off matrix: Cost, Latency, Complexity, Maintainability.
- Present recommendation first, followed by key evidence.
