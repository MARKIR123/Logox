# Academic Paper Writing SOP (学术论文与研究报告严谨撰写 SOP)

## 1. Abstract & Introduction Formula
- State the concrete problem and why existing approaches fall short in 2 sentences.
- State your core insight and technical mechanism in 1 sentence.
- Summarize 3 verified, quantitative contributions.

## 2. Methodology & Formal Notation
- Define all symbols in an explicit notation table or introductory paragraph before using them in equations.
- Maintain mathematical consistency: vectors bold lowercase (`\mathbf{x}`), matrices bold uppercase (`\mathbf{W}`), sets calligraphic (`\mathcal{S}`).

## 3. Evidence-Anchored Discussion
- Every claim in the results section must cite a specific table, figure, or ablation experiment.
- Explicitly state negative results, failure modes, and hardware/compute boundaries.
